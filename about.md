## Hi I'm Kazimierz Biskup

I’m a System Administrator and Cybersecurity Specialist with over 17 years of professional experience.

Throughout my career, I’ve always approached projects with security and automation as core principles. I believe these two areas are most effective when combined. Automation allows configurations to be applied consistently and in a repeatable way, which is very important from a security perspective.

My professional experience has been primarily focused on Windows systems, but I also work extensively with Linux in both professional and personal environments. Although Windows has been a major part of my career, I really enjoy working with Linux and open source software.

When learning new topics, I tend to go deep to better understand how things work under the hood. I’ve found that this mindset is especially valuable when things go wrong and require debugging.

In my spare time, I enjoy experimenting with new tools and technologies and diving deeper into operating system internals.

This blog is a collection of my thoughts, side projects, and experiments. All content is personal and does not reflect the views of my current, future, or past employers.
